A COLLABORATION, STEP BY STEP

Good work starts
before the box ships.

A written scope protects both sides. It clarifies what can be tested, what evidence will be collected, who owns the device, and how any public report will be handled.

  1. 01

    Fit the product to a real test

    Share the product, revision, intended use, and the question you would like evaluated. We confirm the available system, connections, software, and test scope before making commitments.

    PRODUCT + QUESTION
  2. 02

    Agree on scope and sample terms

    Define the procedure, expected evidence, schedule, sample ownership, shipping, return obligations, payment, subscriptions, and any requested disclosure in writing.

    WRITTEN TERMS
  3. 03

    Run the agreed evaluation

    Document the unit and test environment. Keep raw observations. Note failures and interruptions. No unapproved firmware changes, destructive tests, or workload additions.

    DOCUMENTED METHOD
  4. 04

    Deliver the report

    Receive a structured written evaluation with the setup, methods, findings, limitations, and relevant evidence. Any optional public report is discussed in advance and remains editorially independent.

WHAT A COLLABORATION CAN INCLUDE

A clear record
from setup to findings.

The exact deliverables depend on product fit and agreed scope. No reach, review date, or public coverage is promised before discussion.

A /

Pre-test scope

Question, test matrix, supported modes, host requirements, stop criteria, and evidence plan agreed before testing.

B /

Practical evaluation

Setup notes, compatibility and reliability observations, and relevant quantitative measurements where equipment and procedures support them.

C /

Written findings

Structured results, configuration context, source-linked charts or logs when appropriate, issues encountered, and the limitations that matter.

D /

Disclosure record

Product source, loan/gift/return/payment terms, manufacturer involvement, affiliate links, and any material corrections recorded transparently.

↳

Not included by default: guaranteed coverage, guaranteed publication date, guaranteed favorable verdict, audience reach, social posts, positive testimonials, or endorsement.

TERMS THAT KEEP IT STRAIGHT

Independence
is part of the scope.

  • A complimentary sample may be considered when no purchase, deposit, shipping charge, paid API access, or new subscription is required.
  • Any return requirement, loan period, shipping responsibility, payment, affiliate arrangement, and publication request is agreed in writing first.
  • A provided product does not buy a positive conclusion. Factual review can be offered; editorial control remains independent.
  • Private or confidential information is not published without explicit agreement. Public technical evidence is reviewed for privacy before release.
  • Any product source and material relationship are disclosed in the report.
