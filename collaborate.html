TERMS THAT KEEP IT STRAIGHT

Independence
is part of the scope.

  • Preferred arrangement: a complimentary, free-to-keep evaluation unit with shipping covered by the manufacturer.
  • Loan or return terms, shipping responsibilities, schedule, and disclosure are agreed in writing before shipment.
  • A provided product does not buy a positive conclusion. Factual review can be offered; editorial control remains independent.
  • Manufacturer-confidential information is handled only under agreed terms; public evidence is reviewed for privacy before release.
  • Any product source and material relationship are disclosed in the report.
